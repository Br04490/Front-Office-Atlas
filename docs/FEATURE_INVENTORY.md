# Feature inventory — Crystal Design V1

## Persistent shell

Translucent top bar with six navigation controls, search, notifications, settings, and FO avatar. Permanent left rail with Home, Teams, Roster, Trends, Lineups, Games, Possess, Coach, and Settings. The four undeveloped top controls show short informational toasts where appropriate.

## Home

Crystal hero, decorative sphere, data-source status, six clickable module cards, and a 30-team grid. The status panel explicitly identifies unconnected feeds.

## Directory

30 alphabetized franchise cards, each with a local logo. Conference and division filters compose with text search; a division selects its conference. Dynamic count and empty state are present.

## Franchise portal

Official logo, conference, division, accent color, watermark, three status pills, a 30-team selector, and six tabs. Houston is the default. Team choice persists across tab changes during the current page session.

## Tabs

1. **Roster:** 18 interactive silhouette placeholders and pending-source notice. The eventual roster component should follow verified roster size.
2. **Season Trends:** four empty KPI cards, Last 5 / Last 10 controls, illustrative wave, and evidence-first recommendation.
3. **Lineups:** four empty KPIs, illustrative possession window, and context recommendation.
4. **Games & Box Scores:** four unconnected KPIs and future schedule/box-score region.
5. **Possession Windows:** four empty KPIs, no inferred timings, and separated time/possession framework.
6. **Coaching Reasoning:** empty evidence log and a recommendation framework without invented interviews.

## Deferred phases

Four verified Houston player photo cards after visual approval; all-roster ingestion after card approval; deeper analytics, contracts, and trade features later. No backend or live feed is connected.
