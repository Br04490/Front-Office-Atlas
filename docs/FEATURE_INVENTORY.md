# Feature inventory — Crystal Design V1

## Persistent shell

Translucent top bar with six navigation controls, search, notifications, settings, and FO avatar. Permanent left rail with Home, Teams, Roster, Trends, Lineups, Games, Possess, Coach, and Settings. The four undeveloped top controls show short informational toasts where appropriate.

## Home

Crystal hero, decorative sphere, data-source status, six clickable workspace cards, and a 30-team grid. The status panel explicitly identifies unconnected feeds. The original homepage card layout and styling remain; the six labels, subtitles, icons, and destinations changed.

## Directory

30 alphabetized franchise cards, each with a local logo. Conference and division filters compose with text search; a division selects its conference. Dynamic count and empty state are present.

## Franchise portal

Official logo, conference, division, accent color, watermark, three status pills, a 30-team selector, six original tabs, and Team BRI Impact. Houston is the default. Team choice persists across tab changes during the current page session.

## Tabs

1. **Roster:** Actual current NBA.com and NBA Stats roster size (17 or 18), verified official player thumbnails, and name-and-number placeholders where the CDN image is outdated or missing. Player details show NBA ID and verification status.
2. **Season Trends:** four empty KPI cards, Last 5 / Last 10 controls, illustrative wave, and evidence-first recommendation.
3. **Lineups:** four empty KPIs, illustrative possession window, and context recommendation.
4. **Games & Box Scores:** four unconnected KPIs and future schedule/box-score region.
5. **Possession Windows:** four empty KPIs, no inferred timings, and separated time/possession framework.
6. **Coaching Reasoning:** empty evidence log and a recommendation framework without invented interviews.
7. **Team BRI Impact:** entry to the league-wide BRI Calculator with the selected franchise carried as context.

## Six internal workspaces

1. **Automated Sheets:** the seven original sections plus Official Form Library & Generator. The registry cites the published 2023 CBA exhibits and distinguishes unavailable official form population from available Atlas supporting worksheets. No official submission.
2. **Ask CBA:** seven sections, question capture, source/evidence placeholders, and links to related workspaces. A specific proposed action can offer temporary preparation. No rule engine or legal conclusion.
3. **BRI Calculator:** eight sections and user-entered hypothetical BRI growth arithmetic. No NBA cap, tax, or apron projection is represented as official.
4. **Execution Planner:** six internal sections, multi-audience selector, evidence notes, presentation styles, editable briefing drafts, real PowerPoint export, and opt-in temporary server-side supporting packets with review/Save/Discard. Private RAG, video, official forms, and transaction validation remain unavailable.
5. **Intelligence Lab:** ten sections and a query form. Current NBA player search uses the shared verified roster/image record; commercial and other unconnected data sources are explicitly marked unavailable. Trade Case Builder can initiate an opt-in supporting packet.
6. **Scenario Lab:** eight sections with 18 locked one-click templates. Launching creates an editable working copy that can be saved, duplicated, or reset without changing the original. Working copies are stored in browser local storage; an actionable copy can offer temporary supporting paperwork.

## Deferred phases

Deeper analytics, contracts, and trade features remain deferred. No backend or live feed is connected. Player and photo data is a November 15, 2026 snapshot, with unresolved items documented in `outputs/nba-player-photo-audit.md`.
